The maximum value obtained for a nanochannel modified with PAH at low pH was 1.7|e|/nm2, which is similar in magnitude to the native negative charge of the nanochannel.  On the other hand, the maximum negative surface charge obtained after the modification at high pH was -1.1|e|/nm2  which is lower than the initial surface charge of the nanochannel.

The behavior of the rectification factor with pH showed an extended low rectification region between pH 5 and 8. The PNP model was then used to correlate a rectification factors with a certain surface charge density, finding that this region has a net charge near zero. This is why we use the term isoelectric region (IR) (Fig. 3) to name the current-voltage response within this range of pH.
